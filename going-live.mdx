Before you point real customers at Cortex, run through this checklist.

## Pre-launch checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Place a test call">
    Call your Cortex number and run a normal order or question. Confirm the
    greeting, voice, and answers sound right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your hours">
    Make sure [store hours](/voice-core/store-hours) and any holiday closures
    are correct — Cortex uses them to answer "are you open?" and to gate
    ordering.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spot-check the menu">
    If ordering is on, order a few items and confirm they land in your POS with
    the right modifiers and price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review FAQs">
    Confirm the [FAQs](/voice-core/faqs) cover your most common questions:
    location, parking, allergens, delivery radius.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switch on call forwarding

When everything checks out, forward your restaurant line to your Cortex number.

<Info>
  The exact forwarding steps depend on your phone carrier. Most carriers use a
  star code like `*72<number>` to enable and `*73` to disable. Check your
  carrier or ask us at [support@cor-tex.solutions](mailto:support@cor-tex.solutions).
</Info>

<Check>
  You're live. Cortex now answers every forwarded call, 24/7.
</Check>

## Turning Cortex off

You stay in control. Disable call forwarding at your carrier, or toggle the
agent off in the dashboard, any time.
