Before you point real customers at Cortex, run through this checklist.

## Pre-launch checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Place a test call">
    The onboarding wizard's **Test the Agent** step shows your restaurant's
    number and detects your first call automatically. Run a normal order or
    question and confirm the greeting, voice, and answers sound right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your hours">
    Make sure [store hours](/voice-core/store-hours), holiday closures, and
    **order rules** are correct — Cortex uses them to answer "are you open?"
    and to gate ordering.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spot-check the menu">
    Order a few items and confirm they come through with the right modifiers
    and prices. Remember: menu edits only reach the agent after
    [**Deploy to Voice Agent**](/orders/menu).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review FAQs">
    Confirm the [FAQs](/voice-core/faqs) cover your most common questions:
    location, parking, allergens, delivery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Manager PIN">
    Staff need it for pause, transfer, and emergency stop during service. Set
    it in [Settings](/dashboard/settings).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Activate AI ordering

When everything checks out, finish the wizard's **Go Live** step — **Activate
AI Ordering** opens your restaurant and the agent starts taking orders.

If you're keeping an existing number and forwarding it to Cortex, switch
forwarding on at your carrier now.

<Info>
  The exact forwarding steps depend on your carrier. Most use a star code like
  `*72<number>` to enable and `*73` to disable. Not sure? Ask us at
  [cortextechnologyai@gmail.com](mailto:cortextechnologyai@gmail.com).
</Info>

<Check>
  You're live. Cortex now answers every call, 24/7.
</Check>

## Turning Cortex off

You stay in control. Set your [Operational Status](/dashboard/settings) to
**Closed**, pause the agent from [In-Store Mode](/dashboard/in-store-mode), or
disable call forwarding at your carrier — any time.
