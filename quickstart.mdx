This guide takes you from zero to a live agent answering calls.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.cor-tex.solutions](https://app.cor-tex.solutions) and finish
    the onboarding wizard. You'll confirm your restaurant name, address, and
    hours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your phone number">
    Choose one:
    - **Get a new number** — Cortex provisions a local number instantly.
    - **Forward your existing line** — keep your current number and forward
      calls to Cortex.

    See [Voice Core Overview](/voice-core/overview) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your agent">
    In the dashboard, set your [voice and personality](/voice-core/voice-configuration),
    [store hours](/voice-core/store-hours), and [FAQs](/voice-core/faqs).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your menu (optional)">
    If you want Cortex to take orders, connect your
    [POS](/orders/pos) or [load your menu manually](/orders/menu).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test, then go live">
    Place a test call to your agent. When it sounds right,
    [switch on call forwarding](/going-live).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Once forwarding is on, every call to your restaurant is answered by Cortex —
  even during the dinner rush.
</Check>
