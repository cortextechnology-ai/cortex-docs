This guide takes you from zero to a live agent answering calls.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.cor-tex.solutions](https://app.cor-tex.solutions) with
    your work email, then fill in the short intake form — restaurant name,
    your name, and phone. The Cortex team reviews and approves new accounts;
    see [Getting Access](/dashboard/getting-access).
  </Step>
  <Step title="We set up your phone number">
    Phone numbers are provisioned by our team — a new local number, or porting
    / forwarding your existing line. Nothing for you to configure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the onboarding wizard">
    On first sign-in, a six-step setup walks you through your **business
    profile**, **business hours**, **order rules**, and **menu & FAQ**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your agent">
    Fine-tune the [greeting and personality](/voice-core/voice-configuration),
    [store hours](/voice-core/store-hours), and [FAQs](/voice-core/faqs).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your menu">
    Build your menu in [Menu & FAQ](/orders/menu) and hit **Deploy to Voice
    Agent**, or have us connect your [POS](/orders/pos).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test, then go live">
    The wizard's **Test the Agent** step shows your number — call it and place
    a real order. When it sounds right, hit **Activate AI Ordering** in the
    **Go Live** step. See [Going Live](/going-live).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Once you're live, every call to your Cortex number is answered by the AI —
  even during the dinner rush.
</Check>
