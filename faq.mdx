<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What does Cortex do?">
    Cortex is a voice AI that answers your restaurant's phone. It takes
    orders, answers common questions, and pushes orders to your POS 24/7 — so
    you never miss a call during a rush or after hours.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to replace my phone number?">
    No. You can keep your existing number — we port it or you forward it to
    Cortex — or get a new Cortex number. Number setup is handled by our team.
    See [Voice Core Overview](/voice-core/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Cortex work with my POS?">
    Cortex integrates with Clover, Toast, and Square, and works without a POS
    via the [dashboard order queue](/dashboard/orders). Tell us what you run
    and we'll confirm the path. See [POS Integration](/orders/pos).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does setup take?">
    After your account is approved, the guided onboarding takes about 15
    minutes; adding your full menu takes a bit longer depending on its size.
    See the [Quickstart](/quickstart).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does my account say it's under review?">
    New accounts are approved by the Cortex team before your restaurant is
    activated — this is when we set up your phone number. You'll get access as
    soon as it's approved. See [Getting Access](/dashboard/getting-access).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I turn it off?">
    Anytime. Pause the agent from
    [In-Store Mode](/dashboard/in-store-mode), set your
    [Operational Status](/dashboard/settings) to Closed, or disable call
    forwarding at your carrier. You stay in control.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I'm closed?">
    The AI still answers 24/7, but uses your
    [store hours](/voice-core/store-hours) and order rules to tell callers
    you're closed and won't take orders you can't fill.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are calls recorded?">
    Yes — callers hear a recording notice at the start of each call, and you
    can play back, share, or download recordings from the
    [Calls page](/dashboard/calls). Recordings are stored for 90 days.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Still need help?">
    Open a [support ticket](/dashboard/support) in the dashboard, or email
    [cortextechnologyai@gmail.com](mailto:cortextechnologyai@gmail.com) —
    we're happy to help with setup, integrations, or anything else.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
