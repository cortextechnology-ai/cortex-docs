<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What does Cortex do?">
    Cortex is a voice AI that answers your restaurant's phone. It takes orders,
    answers common questions, and manages bookings 24/7 — so you never miss a
    call during a rush or after hours.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to replace my phone number?">
    No. You can keep your existing number and forward it to Cortex, or get a new
    Cortex number. See [Voice Core Overview](/voice-core/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Cortex work with my POS?">
    Cortex integrates with major POS platforms, and works without one via
    [manual setup](/orders/menu). Tell us what you run and we'll confirm the
    path. See [POS Integration](/orders/pos).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does setup take?">
    Most restaurants are live in about 15 minutes for call answering. Adding
    full ordering takes a bit longer depending on your menu and POS. See the
    [Quickstart](/quickstart).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I turn it off?">
    Anytime. Disable call forwarding at your carrier or toggle the agent off in
    the dashboard. You stay in control.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when I'm closed?">
    Cortex uses your [store hours](/voice-core/store-hours) to tell callers
    you're closed and won't take orders you can't fill.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Still need help?">
    Email us at
    [support@cor-tex.solutions](mailto:support@cor-tex.solutions) — we're happy
    to help with setup, integrations, or anything else.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
