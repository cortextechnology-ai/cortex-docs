FAQs teach your agent the answers to questions that don't need a human. Every
FAQ you add is one less interruption for your staff.

## How FAQs work

In the dashboard, open **Menu & FAQ** and switch to the **FAQ** tab. Each
entry is a question and its answer. Cortex matches a caller's question to the
closest FAQ and answers in your agent's voice — no scripts to read, no menus
to press.

## Writing good FAQs

- **One question, one answer.** Don't stuff multiple topics into one entry.
- **Answer the way you'd say it.** Cortex speaks your answer aloud — write for
  the ear, not the page.
- **Keep it current.** Review FAQs when the menu, hours, or policies change.

<Tip>
  Start with the five questions your staff answers most. You can always add
  more.
</Tip>
