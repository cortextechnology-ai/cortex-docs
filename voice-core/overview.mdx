Voice Core is the foundation of every Cortex agent — the phone number, the
greeting, the hours it works, and the questions it can answer. Set this up
first; ordering builds on top of it.

## What's in Voice Core

## Phone number options

- **New number** — we provision a local number for you during setup. Best for
  a clean start or a dedicated ordering line.
- **Keep your existing number** — port it to Cortex, or forward it from your
  carrier. See [Going Live](/going-live) for forwarding steps.

<Info>
  Number provisioning and porting are handled by the Cortex team — you'll see
  your number appear in [Settings → Business Profile](/dashboard/settings)
  once it's ready.
</Info>
