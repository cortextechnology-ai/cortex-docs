title: "Voice Configuration"
description: "Customize your AI agent's greeting and personality."
---

Your agent's first line is the first thing every caller hears. Configure it in
the dashboard under **Settings → AI Agent Voice & Personality**.

## Custom greeting

The opening line of every call. If you leave it blank, the agent uses its
default: *"Hey there — thanks for calling [your restaurant]."*

- Up to **500 characters** — but one short sentence is best.
- The recording notice and the "can I grab a name?" ask are **added
  automatically** — don't write them into your greeting.
- A **Reset to default** link restores the standard greeting anytime.

### Tips for a good greeting

- **Lead with your name.** Callers want to know they reached the right place.
- **Keep it short.** Long greetings get talked over.
- **Set expectations.** *"...I can take your order or answer a quick question."*

## Personality notes

An advanced, optional field (up to **4,000 characters**) that guides the
agent's **tone and word choice** — for example:

> Be extra upbeat and friendly. Mention our daily special if asked what's good.

<Info>
  Personality notes shape *how* the agent talks, not *what* it does. They
  can't change how orders are taken, the menu, or the agent's identity — menu
  and policy live in [Menu & FAQ](/orders/menu) and [FAQs](/voice-core/faqs).
</Info>

## Saving and testing

Hit **Save Agent Settings**, then place a test call. The greeting is the
easiest thing to get subtly wrong — and the easiest to fix.

<Tip>
  After any change, call your own number. Thirty seconds of listening beats
  ten minutes of rereading the text box.
</Tip>
