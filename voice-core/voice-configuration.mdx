Your agent's voice is the first thing every caller hears. Configure it in the
dashboard under **Voice Configuration**.

## What you can set

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Voice" icon="waveform">
    Choose from a library of natural, low-latency voices. Pick one that matches
    your restaurant's vibe.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Greeting" icon="hand">
    The first line callers hear, e.g. *"Thanks for calling Mario's — how can I
    help?"*
  </Card>
  <Card title="Personality" icon="sparkles">
    Tone and style: warm and casual, brisk and efficient, or anything in
    between.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Language" icon="languages">
    Set the primary language your agent speaks.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Tips for a good greeting

- **Lead with your name.** Callers want to know they reached the right place.
- **Keep it short.** One sentence. Long greetings get talked over.
- **Set expectations.** *"...I can take your order or answer a quick question."*

<Tip>
  After any change, place a test call. The greeting and voice are the easiest
  things to get subtly wrong, and the easiest to fix.
</Tip>
