Cortex uses your hours to answer "are you open?", to set expectations on pickup
times, and to gate ordering when you're closed. Keep them accurate.

## Setting regular hours

In the dashboard under **Store Hours**, set open and close times for each day of
the week. You can add multiple windows per day for split shifts (e.g. lunch and
dinner with an afternoon break).

## Special schedules

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Holidays" icon="calendar-off">
    Mark closures so Cortex tells callers you're closed instead of taking orders
    you can't fill.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Special hours" icon="calendar-clock">
    One-off early closes or late opens (events, private parties, weather).
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Warning>
  If ordering is enabled, Cortex will not take orders outside your open hours.
  Double-check holiday closures before long weekends.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  Update special hours as soon as you know them. A wrong "we're open" is worse
  than no answer at all.
</Tip>
