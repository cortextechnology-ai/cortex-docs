Cortex uses your hours to answer "are you open?", to set expectations on
pickup times, and to gate ordering when you're closed. Keep them accurate.
Everything below lives in **Settings**.

## Business Hours

Set open and close times for each day of the week. Multiple windows per day
are supported for split shifts (lunch and dinner with an afternoon break), and
overnight hours work too (e.g. open until 2 AM).

## Order Rules

Control when the agent accepts orders relative to your hours:

- **Buffer Duration** — stop taking orders a set number of minutes before each
  closing time (0–60 minutes, default 15), so the kitchen isn't slammed at
  close.
- **Allow orders before opening** — let early callers place orders for when
  you open.
- **Allow orders after closing** — keep taking orders briefly past close.

A live **Current Order Acceptance** summary shows exactly what your rules add
up to right now.

## After-hours behavior

The AI answers **24/7** — outside your hours it tells callers you're closed
and when you reopen. You can add a custom after-hours message in Settings.

## Manual override

Need to close early or slow things down right now? Set the
[Operational Status](/dashboard/settings) (Open / Busy / Closed / Emergency) —
it temporarily overrides your scheduled hours.

<Warning>
  Cortex will not take orders outside your open hours (plus your order rules).
  Double-check holiday closures before long weekends.
</Warning>
