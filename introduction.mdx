Cortex is voice AI for restaurants. We answer calls, take orders, and manage
bookings 24/7 — so your team can focus on running the floor instead of the phone.

<Info>
  New to Cortex? Start with the [Quickstart](/quickstart) to get your agent live
  in about 15 minutes.
</Info>

## What Cortex does

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Take orders" icon="receipt">
    Answer the phone, take the order, and push it straight to your POS.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Answer questions" icon="messages-square">
    Hours, location, menu, allergens — Cortex handles the calls that never
    needed a human.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manage bookings" icon="calendar">
    Take reservations and confirm them over voice and SMS.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Never miss a call" icon="phone">
    24/7 coverage during rushes, after hours, and on holidays.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Who it's for

Independent restaurants and small groups that lose orders to busy signals,
voicemail, and missed calls. If the phone rings more than your team can answer,
Cortex pays for itself.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your number">
    Get a new Cortex number or forward your existing line.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your agent">
    Set your voice, hours, FAQs, and menu in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Switch on call forwarding and let Cortex pick up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Ready? Head to the [Quickstart](/quickstart).
