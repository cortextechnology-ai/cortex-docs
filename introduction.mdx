Cortex is voice AI for restaurants. We answer calls, take orders, and push
them to your POS 24/7 — so your team can focus on running the floor instead of
the phone.

<Info>
  New to Cortex? Start with the [Quickstart](/quickstart) to get set up, or
  jump straight to the [Dashboard guide](/dashboard/getting-access).
</Info>

## What Cortex does

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Take orders" icon="receipt">
    Answer the phone, take the order with modifiers and upsells, and push it
    straight to your POS.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Answer questions" icon="messages-square">
    Hours, location, menu, allergens — Cortex handles the calls that never
    needed a human.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Run the counter" icon="monitor-play">
    A live ops console shows active calls, today's orders, and alerts — with
    pause, transfer, and emergency controls. See
    [In-Store Mode](/dashboard/in-store-mode).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Never miss a call" icon="phone">
    24/7 coverage during rushes, after hours, and on holidays.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Who it's for

Independent restaurants and small groups that lose orders to busy signals,
voicemail, and missed calls. If the phone rings more than your team can answer,
Cortex pays for itself.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.cor-tex.solutions](https://app.cor-tex.solutions); our team
    approves your restaurant and sets up your phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your agent">
    Set your greeting, hours, FAQs, and menu in the
    [dashboard](/dashboard/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Place a test call, then activate AI ordering and let Cortex pick up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Ready? Head to the [Quickstart](/quickstart).
