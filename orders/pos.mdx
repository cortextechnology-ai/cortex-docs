Connecting your POS lets Cortex sync your menu and push orders in
automatically — no re-keying, no separate tablet to watch.

## Choosing an integration path

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Direct POS integration" icon="plug">
    Orders flow straight into your POS and onto the kitchen line. Menu syncs
    automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manual setup (no POS)" icon="pencil" href="/orders/menu">
    Cortex sends each order to you directly. Best if your POS isn't supported
    yet.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Supported systems

Cortex integrates with major restaurant POS platforms. Support and setup steps
vary by provider — tell us what you run during onboarding and we'll confirm the
path and handle the connection.

<Info>
  Don't see your POS, or not sure? Reach out at
  [support@cor-tex.solutions](mailto:support@cor-tex.solutions). We're adding
  integrations continuously, and manual setup works with any kitchen in the
  meantime.
</Info>

## After connecting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify the menu synced">
    Check that items, prices, and modifiers match your POS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place a test order">
    Confirm it appears in your POS with the correct details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Once orders land cleanly, follow [Going Live](/going-live).
  </Step>
</Steps>
