Connecting your POS lets Cortex push orders in automatically — no re-keying,
no separate tablet to watch.

## Choosing an integration path

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Direct POS integration" icon="plug">
    Orders flow straight into your POS and onto the kitchen line.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dashboard only (no POS)" icon="monitor">
    Orders appear live in the [dashboard](/dashboard/orders) and the
    [In-Store console](/dashboard/in-store-mode). Best if your POS isn't
    supported yet.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Supported systems

Cortex currently integrates with **Clover**, **Toast**, and **Square**. The
connection is set up by the Cortex team during onboarding — tell us what you
run and we'll handle it.

<Info>
  Don't see your POS, or not sure? Reach out at
  [cortextechnologyai@gmail.com](mailto:cortextechnologyai@gmail.com). We're
  adding integrations continuously, and the dashboard flow works with any
  kitchen in the meantime.
</Info>

## How POS orders behave

Orders that flow through your POS are marked with a **POS source** in the
dashboard, and their status is **controlled by the POS** — you'll see them in
the [Orders page](/dashboard/orders) for visibility, but the kitchen lifecycle
lives in your POS.

## After connecting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify the menu">
    Check that items, prices, and modifiers in [Menu & FAQ](/orders/menu)
    match your POS, then **Deploy to Voice Agent**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place a test order">
    Confirm it appears in your POS with the correct details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Once orders land cleanly, follow [Going Live](/going-live).
  </Step>
</Steps>
