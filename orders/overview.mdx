With ordering enabled, Cortex takes orders over the phone and sends them
straight to your kitchen — either through your POS or as a ticket you receive
directly.

## How ordering works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Caller places an order">
    Cortex confirms items, modifiers, and quantities in natural conversation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order is confirmed">
    The agent reads back the order and total before finalizing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order reaches you">
    It lands in your [POS](/orders/pos) automatically, or as a direct ticket if
    you're running [manual setup](/orders/menu).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prerequisites

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="A configured menu" icon="book-open" href="/orders/menu">
    Cortex needs to know your items, prices, and modifiers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A POS connection (optional)" icon="plug" href="/orders/pos">
    Connect your POS for automatic order injection, or run manual.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Info>
  Not ready for full ordering? Cortex can still answer calls and questions with
  [Voice Core](/voice-core/overview) alone. Add ordering whenever you like.
</Info>
