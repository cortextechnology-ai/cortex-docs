With ordering enabled, Cortex takes orders over the phone and sends them
straight to your kitchen — into your POS, or onto the
[Orders page](/dashboard/orders) and the
[In-Store console](/dashboard/in-store-mode) your team watches.

## How ordering works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Caller places an order">
    Cortex confirms items, modifiers, and quantities in natural conversation —
    and upsells where it makes sense.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order is confirmed">
    The agent reads back the order and total before finalizing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order reaches you">
    It lands in your [POS](/orders/pos) automatically, or appears instantly in
    the dashboard's [order queue](/dashboard/orders) with full call context.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Prerequisites

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="A deployed menu" icon="book-open" href="/orders/menu">
    Cortex needs to know your items, prices, and modifiers — built in the
    dashboard and deployed to the agent.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A POS connection (optional)" icon="plug" href="/orders/pos">
    Connect your POS for automatic order injection, or run from the dashboard.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## When a call needs a human

Orders from low-confidence calls are badged **"Needs Review"** in the
dashboard and raised as alerts in In-Store Mode, so your team double-checks
them before the kitchen fires.

<Info>
  Not ready for full ordering? Cortex can still answer calls and questions
  with [Voice Core](/voice-core/overview) alone. Add ordering whenever you
  like.
</Info>
