Your menu is what Cortex uses to take accurate orders. A clean, well-structured
menu means fewer mistakes and faster calls.

## Where your menu comes from

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="From your POS" icon="plug" href="/orders/pos">
    If you connect a supported POS, your menu syncs automatically — items,
    prices, and modifiers stay in sync.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manual menu" icon="pencil">
    No POS? Enter items, prices, and modifiers directly in the dashboard.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Structuring items

- **Items** — the things people order (e.g. "Margherita Pizza").
- **Modifiers** — choices and add-ons (e.g. size, toppings, "no onions").
- **Prices** — base price plus any modifier pricing.

<Tip>
  Use the names your customers actually say. If everyone calls it "the works,"
  add that as an alias so Cortex recognizes it.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Keep prices current. Cortex reads back the total on every order — a stale
  price is a stale total.
</Warning>
