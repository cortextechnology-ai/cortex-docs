The **Settings** page holds everything about your restaurant and how the agent
behaves. The sections, top to bottom:

## Business Profile

Your restaurant's name, owner email, address, and timezone. Your **phone
number is shown read-only** here — numbers and porting are managed by the
Cortex team (you'll see "pending setup" until yours is provisioned).

## Operational Status

A manual override on top of your scheduled hours:

| Status | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Normal operation — agent answers and takes orders. |
| **Busy** | Agent answers but manages expectations on wait times. |
| **Closed** | Agent answers but won't take orders. |
| **Emergency** | Everything stops. Set from [In-Store Mode](/dashboard/in-store-mode); cleared from the red banner with the Manager PIN. |

You can attach a **custom message** for the AI to relay to callers while a
status is active. Operational status temporarily overrides your scheduled
[business hours](/voice-core/store-hours).

## AI Agent Voice & Personality

The agent's greeting and personality notes — covered in detail in
[Voice Configuration](/voice-core/voice-configuration).

## Manager PIN

A numeric PIN (6 digits by default) required for sensitive in-store controls:
pause, transfer, and emergency stop. Only **owners and admins** can set or
update it.

<Warning>
  Set the Manager PIN before your first service — without it, staff can't
  pause the agent or clear an emergency from the floor.
</Warning>

## Business Hours, Special Dates, and Order Rules

Weekly hours (split shifts and overnight supported), holiday closures, and the
rules for when orders are accepted (buffer before close, pre-open and
after-close ordering). All covered in [Store Hours](/voice-core/store-hours).

## After-Hours Behavior

The AI answers calls 24/7. Outside your hours it tells callers you're closed —
optionally with a **custom after-hours message** you set here.

## Support

A preview of your recent support tickets, with shortcuts to create or view
them. See [Support](/dashboard/support).
