The **Analytics** page is the deep-dive view on how phone ordering is
performing.

## Key metrics

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Total Revenue" icon="dollar-sign">
    Revenue from AI-taken phone orders.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Total Orders" icon="receipt">
    Order volume over the analysis window.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Avg Order Value" icon="calculator">
    How much the average phone order is worth — watch this rise as the agent
    upsells.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Conversion Rate" icon="percent">
    The share of answered calls that turn into orders.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Item performance

The **Item Performance** panel ranks your menu items by phone-order volume and
revenue — your best and worst sellers over the line.

<Tip>
  Pair this with the highlights on the [Dashboard Home](/dashboard/overview)
  (upsell rate, best/worst items) when planning menu or pricing changes. If an
  item never sells by phone, check how it's
  [named in your menu](/orders/menu) — callers may be asking for it in
  different words.
</Tip>
