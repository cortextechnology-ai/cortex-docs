The Cortex Dashboard at [app.cor-tex.solutions](https://app.cor-tex.solutions)
is where you run everything — orders, calls, menu, hours, and your AI agent's
settings. Sign-in is secured with Auth0.

## Creating your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Go to [app.cor-tex.solutions](https://app.cor-tex.solutions), choose
    **Create one**, and continue with Auth0 using your work email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us about your restaurant">
    On first sign-in you'll see a short intake form: restaurant name, your
    name, and an optional restaurant phone number. Submit it for approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approval">
    Your account shows **"Your account is under review"** while the Cortex team
    sets up your restaurant. No action is needed from you — once approved,
    sign in again and you'll land on your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Onboarding">
    A guided setup walks you through six steps: business profile, business
    hours, order rules, menu & FAQ, a test call, and going live. See the
    [Quickstart](/quickstart).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Phone number setup (new numbers and porting) is handled by our team after
  signup — you don't need to bring any telephony accounts.
</Info>

## Roles

| Role | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything for their restaurant, including the Manager PIN. |
| **Manager** | Day-to-day operations: orders, calls, menu, settings. |
| **Staff** | Operational views and in-store controls. |
| **Admin** | Cortex platform team — manages restaurants, users, phone setup, and POS connections. |

Every account is scoped to its restaurant: you only see your own calls,
orders, and settings.

## Signed in but seeing "Access Restricted"?

That means your account isn't registered or approved yet. If you've already
submitted the intake form, approval is usually quick — otherwise email
[cortextechnologyai@gmail.com](mailto:cortextechnologyai@gmail.com).
