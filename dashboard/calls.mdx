The **Calls** page is the full history of your phone line: every answered,
missed, and abandoned call, with transcripts and recordings.

## The calls list

Stat cards up top show **Total Calls**, **Answered**, **Missed**, **With
Orders**, and **Total Minutes**. Below, the table lists each call's phone
number, result (Completed, Missed, Abandoned, Voicemail — live calls pulse),
duration, summary, linked order, and time.

- **Search** by phone number, summary, or transcript text.
- **Filter** by result, whether an order was placed, and date range.
- **Export CSV** downloads the filtered list (date, phone, result, duration,
  order ID, summary).

## Call detail

Click any call to open it. Four tabs:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Summary" icon="file-text">
    Phone, duration, call time, and the AI's confidence score, plus a written
    summary and a link to the order if one was placed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Transcript" icon="message-square-text">
    The full conversation, line by line, labeled AI and Caller.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Recording" icon="volume-2">
    The original call audio with a player, **Share Link**, and **Download
    Recording**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Order details" icon="receipt">
    The linked order, or a note that no order was placed.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Info>
  Recordings are stored for **90 days**.
</Info>

## Correcting a call's outcome

On the Summary tab you can edit the **call disposition** — change the result
and, for failed calls, record a failure reason (No Answer, Customer Hung Up,
Technical Issue, Wrong Number, Other). This keeps your analytics honest.
