The **Overview** page is your home screen — a live read on how your AI phone
agent is performing.

  <img src="/images/dashboard-overview.png" alt="The Cortex dashboard Overview page showing KPI cards, the 7-day performance chart, and recent activity" />
</Frame>

## What's on it

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="System status" icon="activity">
    A status pill shows whether the agent is **Active**, **Closed**, **After
    Hours**, **Busy**, **Paused**, or in **Emergency Stop**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="KPI cards" icon="trending-up">
    **Revenue**, **Orders**, **Calls**, and **Minutes Used** for your selected
    time range.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Performance chart" icon="chart-line">
    Daily performance over the selected range, plus a recent-orders activity
    feed.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Highlights" icon="sparkles">
    Upsell rate, conversion rate, average order value, and your best and worst
    selling items.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Time range

Use the **Time Range** selector to switch between Today, the last 7 days
(default), the last 30 days, or a custom range. All KPIs and charts update to
match.

## Two modes

The dashboard has two faces:

- **Manager Mode** — the analytics view described above. For owners and
  managers checking performance.
- **In-Store Mode** — a full-screen operations console for the counter during
  service: live calls, today's order queue, and alerts. See
  [In-Store Mode](/dashboard/in-store-mode).

Switch anytime with the **Enter In-Store Mode** button in the sidebar.

<Tip>
  Keep In-Store Mode on the counter tablet or kitchen screen during service,
  and use Manager Mode for end-of-day and weekly reviews.
</Tip>
