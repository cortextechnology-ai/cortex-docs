The **Orders** page lists every order your agent has taken, with filters,
status management, and a drill-down into the call behind each order.

## The orders table

Each row shows the **customer** (name and phone), **items**, **total**,
**status**, **payment state**, and **time**. Filter by status (Pending,
Confirmed, Preparing, Ready, Completed, Cancelled), payment (Pending, Paid,
Failed, Refunded), or date range.

Click a status to advance it through the kitchen flow: Confirmed → Preparing →
Ready → Completed.

<Info>
  Orders that came in through your POS show **"Status controlled by POS"** —
  their lifecycle is managed on the POS side. See
  [POS Integration](/orders/pos).
</Info>

## Order detail

Expand any order to see everything about it:

- **Customer** — returning customer or first-time caller.
- **Items ordered** — with modifiers and per-item pricing.
- **Order timeline** — every status change, timestamped.
- **Pricing** — subtotal, tax & fees, total, payment method and status.
- **Call summary and link** — the call behind the order, with **View Call
  Transcript** and **Listen to Recording** shortcuts.

## Needs Review

Orders from low-confidence calls are badged **"Needs Review"** — give those a
quick check before firing the ticket. They also surface as alerts in
[In-Store Mode](/dashboard/in-store-mode).

## Something wrong with an order?

Use **Report Issue** on the order to open a support ticket with the order and
call context already attached. See [Support](/dashboard/support).
